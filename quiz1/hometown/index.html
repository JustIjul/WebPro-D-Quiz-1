myHometown: Magetan

Magetan is a regency in East Java Province located in the western part, bordering directly on Central Java.

Known as "The Sunset of East Java", Magetan offers a cool mountain atmosphere, as part of the region lies on the slopes of Mount Lawu. Magetan is renowned for its beautiful natural scenery and distinctive leather handicrafts.
